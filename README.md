### 🤝 Collaboration

This project represents our collaborative exploration of regression modeling, feature engineering, hyperparameter optimization, cross-validation, and ensemble learning.

Our experiments demonstrate the importance of reproducible evaluation, understanding model errors, and selecting models based on evidence rather than complexity.

**Built with Python, Scikit-learn, CatBoost, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Google Colab.**
